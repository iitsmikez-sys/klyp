FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before joining the waitlist.

Who is Klyp for?
Klyp is built for streamers, podcasters, YouTubers, coaches, and anyone producing long-form video content who wants to grow on short-form platforms without spending hours in an editor.
When does Klyp launch?
We're targeting a closed beta in Q3 2025, followed by a public launch shortly after. Waitlist members get early access and locked-in founding member pricing.
How much will it cost?
Klyp will offer a free tier with generous limits, plus paid plans starting around $29/month. Waitlist members will receive a special founding rate that's locked in forever.
What platforms does Klyp export to?
TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, and X (Twitter) — with the correct aspect ratio and format for each, automatically.
How is Klyp different from Opus Clip?
Klyp focuses on deeper hook intelligence, built-in viral scoring that learns from your audience's behavior, and a tighter workflow designed specifically for streamers and podcasters.
